# Apex Sports Intelligence — 1 Minute Video Script

---

## Video Structure

```
0:00 - 0:10 → The Problem (hook)
0:10 - 0:25 → The Three Questions
0:25 - 0:45 → The Solution (product demo)
0:45 - 0:55 → The Technology
0:55 - 1:00 → The Closer
```

---

## Full Script

---

**[ 0:00 — 0:10 ] THE HOOK**

*Screen is black. Single stat appears.*

> **"68% of NBA injuries are preceded by workload spikes that go undetected."**

*Cut to a coach on the sideline, looking frustrated. Cut to a player holding their hamstring. Cut to a commentator saying "they've got all the momentum."*

**Voiceover:**
> "Sports teams make million dollar decisions every single day. On gut feeling."

---

**[ 0:10 — 0:25 ] THE THREE QUESTIONS**

*Three questions appear one by one on screen with sharp sound effects*

> **"Who is about to get injured?"**
> **"Did the coach actually make the right call?"**
> **"Is momentum even real?"**

**Voiceover:**
> "Every coach, every trainer, every front office asks these three questions. Nobody has been able to answer them. Until now."

---

**[ 0:25 — 0:45 ] THE PRODUCT**

*Phone screen appears. App opens.*

**Voiceover:**
> "Meet Apex."

*Cut to Injury Risk screen. Risk score of 84 shown in red.*

> "Apex compares every player to themselves — not the league average. When a player's workload spikes beyond their personal baseline, we catch it before it becomes an injury."

*Risk score circle animates. Red zone badge appears. Plain English explanation fades in.*

*Cut to Coach Leaderboard screen.*

> "Every coaching decision is graded on expected value — not outcome. The right call on fourth and one is still right, even if the play fails."

*Green and red decisions scroll by. EV rate percentages visible.*

*Cut to Momentum Overview screen. Verdict card appears.*

> "And momentum? We tested it. With a Cox proportional hazard model across thousands of games. Hockey — real. Baseball — myth. Now you know."

*Sport comparison chart animates. Hockey bar longest. Baseball bar near zero.*

---

**[ 0:45 — 0:55 ] THE TECHNOLOGY**

*Clean split screen. Phone on left. Stats on right.*

**Voiceover:**
> "Real data. Real math. Free APIs covering NBA, NFL, and MLB. Z-score deviation models. Win probability engines. Statistical significance — explained in plain English for everyone in the room."

*Story Mode paragraph fades in on screen.*

> "From athletic trainers to coaches to fans — everyone gets the same intelligence, in the language they understand."

---

**[ 0:55 — 1:00 ] THE CLOSER**

*Screen goes dark. Logo appears center.*

**Voiceover:**
> "Apex. Because the best decision was always the one backed by evidence."

*Tagline fades in below logo.*

> **"Real sports intelligence — injury risk, decision quality, and momentum, proven with math."**

*Fade to black.*

---

## Director Notes

```
Tone
└── Confident, clean, no fluff
    Every second earns its place

Visuals to show on screen
├── 0:00 → Black screen, white stat text
├── 0:08 → Real sports footage or stock
│          Injury moment, coach on sideline
├── 0:12 → Three questions, bold white text
│          Black background, sharp cuts between each
├── 0:25 → Phone mockup, app opening animation
├── 0:28 → Injury risk screen
│          Risk score 84 in red circle
│          Player name and explanation text visible
├── 0:34 → Coach leaderboard
│          Rankings visible, EV rates colored
│          One decision card with green border
├── 0:40 → Momentum verdict card
│          "Momentum is Real" in green
│          Sport comparison horizontal bars
├── 0:47 → Split screen phone and floating stats
│          Z-score, p-value, EV numbers floating
├── 0:52 → Story mode paragraph on screen
│          Readable, no technical jargon visible
└── 0:55 → Logo centered, clean fade

Music
└── Starts silent for hook
    Low tension build from 0:10
    Confident peak at 0:25 when product appears
    Clean resolution at 0:55

Text on screen rules
├── Never more than 8 words at once
├── Bold white on dark background
├── Stats and numbers always larger than surrounding text
└── App screens shown for minimum 4 seconds each

Pacing
├── 0:00 to 0:10 → Slow, deliberate
├── 0:10 to 0:25 → Sharp, punchy cuts
├── 0:25 to 0:45 → Smooth, confident demo flow
├── 0:45 to 0:55 → Fast, technical credibility
└── 0:55 to 1:00 → Slow, memorable closer
```

## The One Line Every Judge Remembers

> **"The right call on fourth and one is still right, even if the play fails."**

```
This line does three things
├── Explains the Decision Quality module instantly
├── Challenges how everyone thinks about sports decisions
└── Makes the viewer feel something

This is the line that wins the room
Make sure it lands clearly
Pause half a second before and after it
```
