Apex Sports Intelligence
Sports Intelligence. Personalized.

Topic: How Apex turns the chaos of sports data into clear, personalized intelligence — without showing a product UI.

Every game dumps noise: box scores, play-by-play, gut feel, highlight reels. Trainers, coaches, analysts, and fans each need a different brief.

Apex is the idea of one personalized intelligence layer with three pillars:
1. Injury Risk — know who is at risk before they get hurt (traffic-light thinking, workload, alerts as concepts).
2. Decisions — grade coaching calls on expected value / pure logic, not only the final score.
3. Momentum — ask whether a run is statistically real or just a story.

Story Mode is the plain-English payoff: the same insight spoken as a shareable brief.

Closing: Apex Sports Intelligence. Clear insights. Personalized.
